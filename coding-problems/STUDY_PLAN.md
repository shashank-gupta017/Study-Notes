# Big Tech Coding Interview Study Plan
## 18-Week Structured Program (1 Hour Daily) - Blind 75 Complete

### Overview
- **Total Duration**: 18 weeks (4.5 months)
- **Daily Time Commitment**: 1 hour (7 hours/week)
- **Target**: FAANG/Big Tech Interviews
- **Approach**: Pattern-based learning + Spaced Repetition
- **Philosophy**: Quality over quantity - master fewer problems deeply
- **Coverage**: Complete Blind 75 + Essential patterns (20 patterns, ~115 problems)

## Daily Routine Template (1 Hour)

### Weekday Routine (60 minutes)

#### Option A: New Problem Day (Day 1-6)
1. **Quick Review** (10 min): Skim yesterday's solution
2. **New Problem Attempt** (35 min):
   - Read & understand (5 min)
   - Attempt solution (25 min)
   - If stuck at 25 min, look at hints
3. **Study Solution** (10 min): If didn't solve, watch/read solution
4. **Update Tracker** (5 min): Log progress

#### Option B: Review Day (Day 7)
1. **Spaced Repetition** (45 min):
   - Redo 2-3 problems from previous weeks
   - Do them from memory without looking
2. **Pattern Review** (10 min): Read PATTERN_GUIDE.md
3. **Update Tracker** (5 min)

### Weekend (Optional: 1.5-2 hours if available)
- Do 2-3 problems instead of 1
- Longer mock interview practice
- Deep dive into difficult problems

## Tips for Success (1 Hour/Day)

1. **Consistency Over Intensity**: 1 hour daily >> 7 hours on Sunday
2. **Time Box Strictly**: 35 min attempt, then look at hints - don't waste time
3. **Master Before Moving**: Better to deeply understand 80 problems than rush through 200
4. **Use Commute Time**: Review patterns/solutions on phone during travel
5. **Weekend Boost**: If possible, do 1.5-2 hours on weekends
6. **Skip Hard Problems Initially**: Focus on Easy/Medium until Week 12
7. **Join Study Group**: Accountability is crucial with limited time
8. **Review > New**: Spaced repetition is your superpower
9. **Track Everything**: Every minute counts - log your progress
10. **Don't Burn Out**: One rest day per week is okay

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

⚠️ **Stuck on Easy for >30 min**: Review fundamentals
⚠️ **Can't recognize patterns**: Study the pattern guide more
⚠️ **Rushing through solutions**: Slow down, understand deeply
⚠️ **Avoiding Hard problems**: Start attempting with hints
⚠️ **Not timing yourself**: Start practicing under pressure

## Adjustments You Can Make

**If you find you have extra time some days:**
- Do 2 Easy problems instead of 1
- Dive deeper into solution variations
- Practice whiteboard coding
- Watch NeetCode video explanations

**If you're falling behind:**
- Skip some Medium problems temporarily
- Focus on high-frequency patterns first
- Extend the plan to 20 weeks
- Quality > Speed always

**If a problem takes >45 minutes:**
- Stop, look at solution
- Understand the approach
- Code it yourself the next day
- Mark for review in 3 days
